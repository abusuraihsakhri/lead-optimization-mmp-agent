Paired Potency Explorer

Lead optimization · measured matched-pair comparison · local browser calculations

Scientific scope. Enter experimentally measured concentrations for two analogues from the same assay and endpoint. This tool calculates pActivity and a fold shift only. It does not recognize matched molecular pairs from structures, predict potency, assess toxicity, validate bioisosteres, or determine patentability. Structural matched-pair status must be confirmed separately.

Compare measured potencies

For concentration-based endpoints, a lower value corresponds to higher apparent potency. Positive ΔpActivity means the analogue has a lower concentration value.

Enter two positive concentrations and select Calculate.

Formula: pActivity = −log₁₀(concentration in mol/L); ΔpActivity = pAnalogue − pReference; fold shift = Reference / Analogue.
